`SseFeature` and `SseHub` serve server-sent events. `hub.publish(message, filter)` queues an event for the connected actors the filter accepts; `SseFeature` serves the hub on an authenticated GET (the `AuthFeature` user, or `actor(c)`) with `text/event-stream`, `Cache-Control: no-cache` and `X-Accel-Buffering: no`, a shared heartbeat (`heartbeatMs`, 15 s), limits per process and per actor, and drops clients that disconnect or fall more than `maxQueuedBytes` behind. Bun closes a connection idle for `idleTimeout` seconds (10 by default), which cut quiet streams: each SSE request gets a timeout of twice the heartbeat, and at least two of Bun's 4-second idle-check steps past it, with `server.timeout()`, and a warning is logged when that is not possible and `idleTimeout` is not longer than the heartbeat. Features get `beforeShutdown()`, which `Kernel.shutdown()` runs once the server stops listening and before it waits for open requests: `SseFeature` ends its streams there, where the wait used to last the whole `shutdownGraceMs`. `HealthCheckFeature.addCheck()` lets a feature add a `/health` check; `SseFeature` reports its client count.
